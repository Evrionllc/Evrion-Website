Evrion07
Case Study · Emberhaus

We build the right way, not the easy way

The Challenge

The most common e-commerce vulnerability is trusting client-supplied prices or treating a redirect as proof of payment. Both are exploitable — and both are tempting because they're easier.

The Approach

  • Every amount recomputed on the server — the cart is convenience, never truth
  • Orders confirmed only by a signature-verified Stripe webhook, never the browser
  • Auth.js sessions, roles, and credentials done properly from day one
  • The secure path is the only path — the architecture you'd ship if real money moved

The Name

Why Evrion

Evrion is inspired by Evren, a word rooted in Turkic mythology — a mighty dragon symbolizing strength, wisdom, and transformation.

Over time it also came to mean the universe itself. That dual meaning — power and possibility, myth and discovery — captures our vision: building technology with the ambition to shape what comes next.
